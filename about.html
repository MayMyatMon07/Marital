About DuBo Martial Arts

DoBu Martial Arts is a marital arts gym,established for providing marital courses to everyone .It offers a range of classes from fitnees training to self-defence courses.The gym provides a range of membership options.They include basic,intermediate,advanced,elite,and junior membership packages.Our aim is to provide quality services for beginners, and people who want to know about marital arts.We help people improve their self-defence skills.

At DoBu Martial Arts, members can choose from a variety of martial arts classes, including Jiu-Jitsu, Judo, Karate and Muay Thai. The gym also provides fitness training and specialist self-defence courses.

Our facilities include a large matted martial arts training area, a fully equipped gym,a sauna and a steam room, as well as changing and shower facilities. We consider our members to have a comfortable envrioment while they are training at the gym.
